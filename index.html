Why AIKids?

AI is the future, and it’s our kids who should benefit from it.

AIKids was founded to educate and inform the next generation about artificial intelligence and its impact on the world around us.

Many of us are learning and adapting to AI as it evolves. Today’s school-age children will be the first generation to grow up in a world where AI is always present.

This creates an incredible opportunity for young people to think creatively, question ideas, and imagine what AI could become. It also brings responsibility. We believe children should learn not only about the possibilities of AI, but also about the risks, ethics, and challenges that come with it.
