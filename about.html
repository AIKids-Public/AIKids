About us

Curious minds, bright futures

AIKids helps children understand artificial intelligence, think creatively about it, and grow up ready to shape it responsibly.

I love asking questions. Let me tell you what AIKids is all about!

Our mission

Helping the next generation understand and shape AI

Today’s school-age children are the first generation to grow up with AI all around them: in their homes, their schools and the apps they use every day.

AIKids exists to make sure they don’t just use AI, but understand it. We create friendly, hands-on activities that explain how AI works, spark creative ideas, and encourage children to ask the big questions about how it should be used.

Our goals

What we want every child to take away

💡

Understand

Learn what AI is, how it learns from data, and where it already shows up in everyday life.

🎨

Create

Use imagination and problem-solving to explore what AI could do, and invent new ideas of their own.

🛡️

Question

Think about fairness, safety, and ethics, so they can use AI wisely and help shape it for good.

Meet Spark

Your friendly AI guide

Spark is the AIKids robot. They love reading, asking “why?”, and solving puzzles. You’ll find Spark across the site and in every activity, sharing tips, asking questions, and cheering you on.

Like all good explorers, Spark knows that AI is a tool that people build. That means people, including you, get to decide what it should be used for.
